# Desktopity

A little smiley who lives on your desktop. He floats around your screen, talks out loud, listens when you speak, and has moods of his own. He's sweet. Mostly.

**[Download for Windows](../../releases/latest/download/Desktopity-Windows.zip)** — free, Windows 10 and 11.

## Opening him the first time

1. Right-click `Desktopity-Windows.zip` → **Extract All**, then double-click `Desktopity.exe`.
2. If you see "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**.
3. Click **Run anyway**. Allow the microphone so he can hear you.

On first launch he downloads his brain once (about 2 GB). After that he runs offline.

## Privacy

Speech recognition and his brain run entirely on your PC. Nothing you say is recorded or uploaded.
